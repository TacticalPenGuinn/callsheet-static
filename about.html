About this demo

This is a static recreation of the Penguinn call sheet, a shop board I originally built in PHP and MySQL. Technicians used it to see open service calls by priority, move work between columns, and pull a simple report.

The live shop app needed a PHP server. This copy is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so it can sit on a static site. The calls, customers, phone numbers, and addresses on these pages are fictional sample data.

You can drag a call to another priority, edit it, close it, or add one. The reports page filters the same sample set. Leaving a page or reloading brings the original sample back. Nothing is stored.

Blue cards are calls waiting on parts or a warranty part. Green, yellow, and red cards are low, medium, and high priority.
